About the Library

The Fitzgerald Knowledge Library is an independent public knowledge project dedicated to collecting, organizing, and sharing useful information. Our goal is to make research easier to find, understand, and verify.

We believe knowledge should be accessible to everyone. Articles should identify their sources and distinguish documented facts from opinions and uncertainty.
